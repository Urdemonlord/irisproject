Privacy policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

IRIS is a self-hosted email service run by Meow Labs. This page explains what IRIS stores about you, why, and for how long. We don’t sell your data and we don’t show ads.

What we store

  • Your mail. Messages in your mailbox are stored on our own mail server so you can read them. We don’t scan them for advertising.
  • Your session. When you sign in we create a session that lasts up to 30 days. We never store your password: sign-in creates a separate mail-server password for that session only, kept encrypted and destroyed when you sign out or the session ends.
  • Temporary inboxes. Each temporary address and its messages live for 1 hour. After it expires, the address stops working immediately and its messages are deleted within about an hour.
  • Abuse protection. To limit how many temporary addresses can be created, we record your IP address and a random browser identifier for each request. These records are deleted after 2 days.
  • Server logs. Our servers keep short technical logs (errors, request outcomes) to keep the service running and secure.

Cookies and local storage

IRIS only sets cookies that are needed for it to work:

Your browser also keeps the current temporary address (session storage, cleared when you close the tab) and your cookie choice (local storage).

If analytics is enabled on this server, we only load it after you allow it in the cookie notice. It counts anonymous page views (for example “/temp”) and never sees message contents, addresses or tokens. You can change your choice at any time: .

Who we share data with

Nobody, unless the law requires it. Mail you receive comes from the senders you gave your address to; mail is delivered by standard internet email.

Your choices

  • Sign out at any time to end your session on that device.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, or to delete your account and mailbox, using the contact below.

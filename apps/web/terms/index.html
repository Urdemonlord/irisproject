The service

IRIS gives account holders a mailbox, and gives anyone a free temporary address that can receive mail for 1 hour by default, extendable up to 24 hours in total. Temporary addresses need no account and no personal details. They are receive-only, and everything in them is deleted after they expire. Don’t use them for anything you need to keep, or for sensitive information.

Your mailbox and its mail are yours. On the instance run by Meow Labs they are stored on our own mail server; if you run your own IRIS instance, they stay on your server. Signing in creates a session that lasts up to 30 days; signing out ends it on that device.

If you break these rules, we may delete the inbox and its mail immediately and block the address, network or account involved.

Limits

  • Up to 10 temporary addresses per network per hour, and 3 active at a time per browser.
  • Temporary inboxes last 1 hour by default and can be extended up to 24 hours in total.
  • Messages up to 25 MB.
